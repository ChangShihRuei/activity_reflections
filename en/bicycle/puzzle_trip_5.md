## 2. Reflection
This was my sixth long-distance cycling trip and my fourth time participating in the Puzzle Island Cycling Trip. We rode from Hualien city to Taitung city along Provincial Highway 9—200 km in total over three days. What stayed with me most was not only the climbs, but also memories of the Grand Tour I did with classmates two years ago. We passed many elementary-school memories and rest stops from that earlier ride, but riding with different people also felt different. I learned a lot on this trip, and it made me cherish cycling with my classmates even more.

As for the climbs, we extended the ride to Taitung this time. Besides the exhausting uphill near Dantung Dafu Forest Park that we had also ridden on the Grand Tour, there was another long climb of nearly two kilometers from the south end of Luye toward Chulu. It really tested muscular endurance. Fortunately, I finished the journey successfully in the end.
