## 2. Reflection
This was my fifth long-distance cycling trip and my third time participating in the Puzzle Island Cycling Trip. I missed the previous one because I went to Korea for FIRA, which was a real pity—I hope I can make it up when I have time. This ride was very long, especially on the first day, when we had to cover nearly 90 km. Still, it was a good way to train my endurance and stamina. What impressed me most was that almost the entire route had gentle climbs, which really tested muscular endurance. Fortunately, I finished the journey successfully in the end.
