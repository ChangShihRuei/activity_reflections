## 1. Event Information

- Date: 20260919
- Venue: College of Science Building, NTNU Gongguan Campus (No. 88, Sec. 4, Tingzhou Rd., Wenshan Dist., Taipei)
- Event: 22nd Taiwan National Geographic Knowledge Contest (2026), Geography Knowledge track
- Stage: City/county semifinal (Taipei City)
- Division: Junior high
- Check-in: 10:00–10:20 (enter room 10:20–10:30)

- Taipei host school: Taipei Municipal Zhongshan Junior High School
